# Title

You can put a bit of flavor text here.

## Requirements

Write down what you need to wear this Hat in here. This will usually be something along the lines of "Received a dot in (Attribute)", but may include in-character actions (signing up for a guild, being bitten by a vampire) or ongoing requirements (follow the principles of your faith, pay your union dues).

## Powers

A list of the special rules that this Hat gives you access to. You might unlock these over time, or have access to them right away.

## Progression

Does this Hat get stronger? If so, how?

## Risks

What challenges do you face because of this Hat? This might take the form of occupational hazards, eldritch corruption, expectations...anything that inconveniences you because you wear this Hat.
