# Thief

You slink through the shadows, rifle through pockets, and disarm the traps people set for people like you.

## Requirements

- Received one dot in Thief or Sneaker
OR
- Successfully steal a valuable object
OR
- Successfully disarm a trap

## Powers

### Traps and Disarming
You are especially good at finding, identifying, and disarming mechanisms designed to hurt trespassers.

To search for traps, declare that you are searching for traps, then roll with Thief, Sneaker, or a Skill connected to technology (such as Engineer). Success means you have clearly identified whether or not there's a trap, as well as how many traps there are and how they are triggered; additional successes create dots of a temporary Source called Thief's Instinct (which can be used with any Thief roll). Failure means that you conclude the *opposite* of what's true--if there's no trap, you've fixated on something as an *obvious* trap, and if there *is* a trap, you're certain there's nothing.

To disarm a trap you've found, declare which trap you are attempting to disarm and how you intend to disarm it. You can always 'disarm' a trap by triggering it intentionally, and this does not require a roll, though it does carry the potential of getting someone hurt or setting off an alarm...not to mention that some traps can be activated multiple times.

If you *do* need to roll to disarm the trap, roll with Thief, Sneaker, or a skill connected to technology (i.e., Engineer). Success means you have disarmed the trap. Additional successes allow you to turn parts of the trap into raw materials that may be useful or valuable, or can be used to generate more Thief's Instinct (t.Source).

### Pickpocketing
Your nimble hands are capable of stealing something right out of a victim's pockets.

To do this, roll with Thief or Sneaker. Success means you're able to get the item out of the pocket without the target noticing you. Failure means you get the thing, *but* the victim notices immediately and is able to connect the theft to you (either because they felt you stick your hand in there, or because you're the last person they bumped into before the item vanished). Additional successes generate Thief's Instinct (t.Source).

### Sneaking Around
You have a knack for fading into the background.

To do this, describe how you're blending in. Maybe you're literally hiding in a dark corner, or maybe you're staying low and slinking around behind some furniture or machinery, or maybe you're disappearing into the crowd.
Then, roll with Sneaker. The GM may ask for a more specific roll depending on what you're doing--for instance, if you're blending into a crowd, the GM might ask for a Smooth Sneaker roll to act natural.

Success means you create dots of a temporary Source called Distance. This can mean literal distance (moving further away from whoever might be tracking you) or figurative distance (making it harder to tie you to whatever you just got away with).

To fully shake whoever might be tailing you, you need to roll with Distance or burn Distance on a roll. You might roll Sneaker Distance to find the ideal hiding spot, for instance, or--if you've got a getaway vehicle--maybe you'd roll Smooth Driver and burn Distance to play a shell game on the highway and get your assailants to mistake someone else's car for yours (or whatever setting-appropriate vehicle you're riding in). You might roll Hacker Distance if you're hiding a digital trail, or Smooth Talker and burn Distance to convince the cops you have an ironclad alibi (not usually a good idea, but this is a fantasy game where impossible things can happen).

## Progression

Thieves get by on cunning, wit, and most importantly, the decisiveness of someone whose hubris is only matched by their skill.

If you accrue 10 dots of Thief's Instinct in a single scene, you can cash them all in for a free dot of Thief or Sneaker, up to your Depth.

## Risks

### Do I Even Need To Say It?
Stealing is usually a crime. This means you face the threat of jailtime if you get caught doing it. Obviously, if you're 'stealing' from a place that's been abandoned for years, you're probably not gonna get in trouble--but if you're lifting keys from the guards, stealing cash money from a bank vault, or committing a wee bit of burglary against the local politicians, you are probably going to be very much wanted for this.
