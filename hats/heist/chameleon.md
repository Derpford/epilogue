# Chameleon

You blend into any crowd, seamlessly fitting into whatever space you need to enter; nobody knows it was you. All they know is that you were polite, courteous, and wholly unmemorable.

## Requirements

- Received one dot in Talker, Executive, Understander, or a similar social Skill.

## Powers

### Social Infiltration

You are especially good at assuming the identities of others.

To disguise yourself, first describe the role you intend to take on. More generic roles like 'construction worker', 'health inspector', 'security guard', etc. are easier; the GM may rule that you must roll with a dice penalty to mimic more specific people or people who have more authority, and may give bonus dice for having the right stuff on hand (you can impersonate a specific security guard really easily if you have their keycard already).

Then, roll with Talker, Executive, Understander, or a similar social Skill. Gain two dots of a temporary Source called Disguise for each success on this roll. Disguise represents your ability to maintain your charade; it can be rolled whenever you need to do something that fits your role, as well as whenever you're trying to convince someone that you do in fact belong here. You can burn Disguise to represent having to hastily exit a conversation before someone catches you in a lie, having to make up some bullshit on the spot, or other similarly risky ploys.

While you have dots of Disguise, your disguise holds up. **Failing a roll with Disguise causes you to lose one dot of Disguise.** When you're out of dots of Disguise, you've been made; whenever you interact with someone, they'll realize that it's just a disguise. You are encouraged to get out of any secure areas you're in before this happens.

### Smooth Talker

While in disguise, you can get people to do and say things they otherwise wouldn't.

To get info you're not supposed to have, explain how you get the other guy talking. Are you trying to get him drunk? Do you want to convince him you're his new best friend? Does he already owe the person you're impersonating? Are you pretending to be new on the job and in need of training? The GM will tell you if you get bonus dice or dice penalties based on how you approach this. Then, roll with Disguise; success means you get the info out of them, with additional successes giving you the ability to ask more questions about your topic of choice--one per additional success.

To convince someone to do something, describe what you want them to do and how you're persuading them, then roll with Disguise. Like with getting info, the GM might tell you that you get a dice bonus or penalty if your approach is just right or just wrong. They may also rule that you don't need to roll, if the role you're playing already has the authority to make it happen (i.e., if you're pretending to be a military base's commanding officer, you can order the soldiers around without a roll).

While non-Chameleons can try to do both of these things, you are more effective than they are. When a die shows a 1 on one of these rolls, count it as two successes.

## Progression

Chameleons are often driven by a desire to show off, to pull off trickier and more impressive acts of hiding in plain sight. When you impersonate a specific person and leave without getting caught, you can gain a dot of Wildcard (Source), up to your Depth. This Source represents your versatility as a 'performer', and can be rolled whenever you need to be mysterious, difficult to detect, or impossible to predict.

## Risks

### It's Not Strictly Illegal, But...
Your skills are somewhat frowned upon in polite society. If someone finds out that you've been using a disguise to fool them, they will at best be very cross with you--and if they think you're committing some sort of crime by doing so, they *will* call the cops. Nobody wants to find out they've been lied to.

### Identity Crisis
As you get better at this, you may find that your *actual* identity becomes more and more difficult to pin down. **If you reach 10 dots in Disguise and stay at 10 dots until the end of a scene**, you start to lose yourself in your role; you must burn a dot of another Source or become an NPC for the next scene. You get a new chance to recover from this at the end of each scene.
