# Pharmacist

You specialize in drugs, medicines, first aid, and various other forms of applied chemistry. Whether they need a kilo of amphetamines or a single dose of adrenalin, you're the guy.

## Requirements

- Received one dot in Doctor or Scientist

## Powers

### Miracle Worker, Sort Of

Given time and materials, you can produce various pharmaceutical chemicals to help your allies.

To make something to help someone, first decide what exactly you're doing. Describe the kind of medicine you're preparing, whether that's a sterile bandage, an antibiotic, a painkiller, or what-have-you, as well as what it's supposed to do. Describe what materials you're working with--these are typically Simple Items, and so you can fold them into the following roll. Using Complex Items for this may convince the GM to give you bonus dice.

Then, roll with Doctor or Scientist.

Success means you have the desired effect, with additional successes amplifying it. For healing, you get 3 points of healing per success. The GM will work out how other effects work, usually by giving out Temporary Sources.

Failure means that your product is fouled up, ineffective, or has nasty side effects. You can choose to give it to the patient anyway, at which point the GM will describe how the medicine hurts as well as heals. **You always get the desired effect; failure means you get unwanted effects too.**

### Moneymaker, Sort Of

You can also make 'pharmaceutical' chemicals to sell for a pretty large payout.

To do this, describe what you're making and why it'll sell. It could be street drugs, or it could be the sort of over-the-counter thing that you *won't* get arrested for selling.

Either way, roll with Doctor or Scientist. Like with medicines for allies, success means you produce something pure, and failure means you made something with side effects. You can still sell it either way, but your customers might not come back, for one reason or another...

Doing this grants you dots of a temporary Source called Product, two dots per one success. You have to find someone to buy it to convert it into Money. (The Broker Hat might be helpful for this.) This Source lasts until you either sell the Product or have it taken from you (either by rival drug dealers or by the cops).

## Progression

Pharmacists have a ton of formal training to go through before they can even call themselves Pharmacists. They do not have any new progression mechanics.

## Risks

### That's How I Lost My Medical License
It turns out the authorities do not look kindly on pharmacists who bend the rules. If you're caught selling Product in ways that violate the law (i.e., most ways you can sell it), you may have your medical license revoked, at which point **you lose this Hat**.

### Side Effects
The risks of side effects cannot be overstated. The GM is free to come up with all sorts of nasty effects, from dizzy spells that randomly ruin your rolls to outright cardiac arrest and death. You can always get the effect you want, but doing it when you fail means that a finger on the monkey's paw curls.

Furthermore, if you *sell* Product that has side effects, you risk pissing off some very powerful, very well-armed people, who aren't going to stop at revoking your medical license.
