# Gunsmith

You are an expert in tuning, tweaking, and using firearms. You know everything there is to know about how to put a bullet in a target from any distance, as well as how to effectively arm your allies.

## Requirements

- Received one dot in Shooter

## Powers

### Tune-Up

You can tweak, maintain, and otherwise mess with someone's gun to make it shoot straighter.

To do this, pick a Simple or Complex Item that is also a firearm. The definition of 'firearm' is setting-dependent--in some settings, this would include laser blasters, and in others it might be limited to cannons and similar.

Then, roll with Shooter. Note down how many successes you got next to the item--for example, rolling 4 successes would turn a Rifle into a Rifle +4.

When someone uses a gun that has been improved this way, they gain bonus dice equal to the successes you got when tweaking the gun. In other words, the Rifle +4 from earlier gives you four extra dice when you try to shoot something with it.

### Poppin' Helmets

You're also especially good at knowing *where* to shoot. When a die shows 1 on a roll to shoot something, you count it as two successes; describe how you nail the target in an especially vulnerable place--a headshot, striking a weak link in a chain, flanking the target to negate their cover, and so on.

## Progression

Gunsmiths pride themselves on their collection. When you procure a Unique Item that is also a firearm, you can pick up a free dot of Armory (Source), up to your Depth. Armory represents your collection of unique and interesting firearms, and can be used in rolls relating to having the right gun for the occasion, convincing someone of your expertise in firearms, or outfitting a group with usable weapons.

## Risks

### Less Than Legal
Depending on the setting and what you're doing, the weapons you collect might not be weapons you're legally allowed to own. You may be raided by the feds if you get careless about who sees you with a weapon, and they're likely going to treat you like the armed and dangerous threat you are.

### More Than Lethal
Guns kill people. You're really good at using them to kill people. While using a firearm, you cannot do nonlethal damage.

Some items are logical exceptions to this rule, such as tasers. You have to use them *correctly*, though. A beanbag round to the skull is still a traumatic cranial injury, and yes, those *also* kill people.

### The Gunslinger's Fate
The nature of weaponry is that picking up a weapon does not make you any safer; it merely gives you the means to defend yourself. You are painfully aware of this. As a result, **when you are brought to 3 or more Wounds by a gunshot, you cannot use Heroic Actions until the next scene.** You're too busy trying to hold your guts in to do anything heroic. Receiving first aid will also clear this effect.
