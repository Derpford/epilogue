# Items Appendix

Most of the time, Simple and Complex Items can simply be handled with common sense, but someone's inevitably gonna ask questions like "How much damage can I do with this chair?" or "What amount of armor does a bulletproof vest give me?". This appendix is for answering those questions.

Just because an item's on this list doesn't mean you can have it. Check with your GM to see if an item is setting-appropriate.
